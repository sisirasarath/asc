CAMPUS SPOTLIGHT

Discover MACE

Achievements and unique student attractions of Mar Athanasius College of Engineering.

Pioneering Legacy

Pioneered engineering education in central Kerala in 1961. Managed by Mar Athanasius College Association and Aided by the Government of Kerala, MACE was the first in Asia under Christian Management.

Advanced Labs & Prototyping

Equipped with state-of-the-art experiential testing and research facilities including the **IoT Lab**, **FAB Lab**, and the **AICTE IDEA Lab** on campus.

Takshak Techno Fest

Home of **Takshak**, MACE's national-level annual techno-cultural festival, which brings together engineering minds across India for competitions, exhibitions, and workshops.

Entrepreneurship Ecosystem

Supported by the **IEDC** (Innovation & Entrepreneurship Development Cell) and **IIC** (Institutions Innovation Council) to spark startups and startup incubation.

Vibrant Campus Life

Home to various student clubs including IEEE MACE, NSS, Sanskriti (Cultural Club), MACE Film Society, Quiz Club, Music Club, and the Literary & Debating Club.

Distinguished Placements

Excellent campus placement drives with top multinational corporations, supported by a strong global Alumni Association representing MACE across the world.
