How It Works

3 Quick Steps
01

Choose Your Platform

Select either Google Drive for bulk document upload or WhatsApp for instant messaging and queries.

02

Share Your Resource

Upload your PDF or images. Please use a clean naming format: Subject_Module_Semester.pdf.

03

Reviewed & Published

The ASC team reviews and categorizes the submission into the MACE Notes Portal to help hundreds of peers.

Submission Guidelines

Upload as PDF format for highest quality across mobile and desktop
Include Subject Name, Code & Semester in the document name
Handwritten notes are strongly encouraged — ensure pages are legible & complete
Follow the 2024 KTU Scheme (or explicitly specify if 2019 scheme)
Module-wise separated files are preferred over one oversized document
Do not upload copyrighted textbooks or paid commercial materials
